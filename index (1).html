What we do

We structure deals, bring investors together and oversee how the capital is governed.

Private Capital Syndication

We mobilize capital through trustee-led syndicates for growth businesses, VC funds and alternative assets. As a fund of funds syndicator and advisor, we enable accredited professionals and institutional investors to access Africa's leading VC and alternative asset funds.

For accredited professionals and institutional investors

Capital Structuring and Startup Advisory

We advise growth businesses on how to raise capital, including the right mix of equity and debt, and prepare them for conversations with investors.

For founders and growth businesses

Technical Advisory

We provide technical advisory services to private institutions, development finance institutions and government agencies to execute high priority projects for SME investor readiness, growth and economic development.

For private institutions, DFIs and government agencies

Venture Building

We build and operate companies that widen access to investing. Today these are Biya, TGI Club and Twelvevest.
